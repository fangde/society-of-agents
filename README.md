# The Society of Agents: How Intelligence Should Be Architected

A note on how learned intelligence should be architected — on end-to-end learning versus
end-to-end architecture, and on why intelligence may be better organized as a society of
semi-autonomous learned systems operating at their own spatial, temporal and informational scales.
